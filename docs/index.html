Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

GridDown is built for the worst moments. Every guide and tool works without internet, without cloud, without your data.

🆘
Emergency Mode — free
One tap (or long-press the app icon) for a large-print crisis screen: severe bleeding, CPR, choking, shock, burns, survival priorities, SOS signaling and live GPS / MGRS coordinates.
📚
315 Guides, 19 Categories — free
Water, fire, shelter, food, medical, health, navigation, comms, security, disaster, grid-down home, terrain, vehicle, power, urban, homesteading, leadership and kids. Each opens with a Quick Glance — what to do right now.
🔗
Sourced, Not Made Up
Every guide lists the public sources it was written from — FEMA, CDC, American Red Cross, NOAA, US Army field manuals. Medical guides awaiting clinician sign-off are clearly badged.
🤖
On-Device AI Advisor
Field Intelligence answers in plain English from GridDown's own guides and shows which guide it used. It runs entirely on your phone — one download over Wi-Fi, then no internet. Pro + AI plan.
🎯
Drills & Readiness
196 scenario quizzes (multiple choice, priority order, decision trees), a Daily Drill, and a 50-question 72-hour readiness scan that ends with your prep plan.
📡
HAM Repeater Database
200+ US repeaters, searchable by state, city or frequency, filterable by ARES, RACES and SKYWARN. Fully offline. Pro plan.
🧭
Offline Tools
Position & waypoints in lat/long, UTM and MGRS, coordinate converter, star map, Morse code with audio and flashlight, radio frequencies, survival and water-purification calculators, knots, signaling.
🌐
Offline Translator
On-device translation for 10 languages — Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Hindi — with emergency phrase cards in large print.
🎒
Plan Ahead
My Kit for bug-out, vehicle, home and medic kits; 25 disaster checklists; step-by-step Real-World Flows; gear inventory with expiration alerts; a Family Emergency Planner with printable cards.
🔒
Zero Data Collection
No account. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. Location is shown, never stored or sent. Backups are a file you export and keep.
Built for Grid-Down

No signal.

Everything ships inside the app. After install you never need a connection again — dead zones, basements, the backcountry, a week-long blackout. The optional AI model and translator languages download once over Wi-Fi, then work offline too.
