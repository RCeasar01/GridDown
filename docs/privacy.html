Privacy Policy

Effective: June 19, 2026 — BannedProduct Media Inc.

1. Overview

GridDown is built on a single privacy principle: we do not collect your data. The app functions entirely offline. No account is required. No data leaves your device for GridDown’s servers — because GridDown has no servers that receive personal data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

GridDown does not collect, transmit, sell, or share: your name, email, location, browsing history, advertising IDs, biometric data, or crash reports. GridDown has no servers and no analytics. The only network traffic the app makes is the short list of one-time downloads and purchase checks in Section 9 — none of them carry anything you type, read, or save.

4. Location Data

GridDown requests location permission to display GPS coordinates on the offline map. Location data is displayed on screen only — never stored, logged, or transmitted. It is accessed only on the Map screen and only while you’re on it.

5. Payments

All purchases are handled by RevenueCat and the platform payment processor (Apple or Google). GridDown never sees your payment information. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID for purchase verification. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

6. On-Device AI

The Field Intelligence Advisor (Pro + AI plan) runs a language model entirely on your device. When you first enable it you choose a model and the app downloads it once (1.1 GB or 2.4 GB) directly from Hugging Face, a public model-hosting service — an ordinary file download with no account and nothing you have typed. After that, your questions, the answers, and the guide text the Advisor reads are processed on your phone and never sent anywhere. Conversations live in device memory during the session and are cleared when you close the chat. You can delete a downloaded model at any time.

7. Translation

The Offline Translator uses Google ML Kit, an on-device library. Language packs (∼30 MB each) are downloaded from Google’s servers under Google’s privacy policy. To manage those downloads, ML Kit creates an anonymous installation identifier for this copy of the app and sends it to Google; it is not linked to you or to any content, and GridDown does not use or store it. Once a pack is downloaded, the text you translate is processed on your phone and never sent to Google or anyone else.

8. Analytics

GridDown integrates no analytics SDK whatsoever. No Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no similar service. Apple and Google may collect standard platform diagnostics per their own policies.

GridDown does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. No account creation is required at any age.

11. Your Rights

Because we do not collect your data on our servers, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or export. For any questions, email privacy@bannedproduct.com.

12. Changes

Material changes will be noted in the app’s update release notes with an updated effective date above.
