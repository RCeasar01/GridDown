## 1. Medical Content — App Store Guideline 5.1.1

GridDown includes emergency preparedness guides covering field medicine (wound care, hypothermia management, improvised splinting, burn treatment, shock protocol, etc.). This content is intended for emergency situations where **professional care is unavailable** — grid-down scenarios, natural disasters, wilderness emergencies, and austere environments.

### Founder Credentials

Content is authored by a founder with the following verified background:
- **101st Airborne Division (Air Assault)** — Screaming Eagles
- **3rd Brigade Combat Team, 187th Infantry Regiment** — Rakkasans
- **8th US Army / 65th Medical Brigade**
- **Combat Infantryman**
- **Combat Lifesaver**
- **Eagle First Responder** — 101st Airborne enhanced Combat Lifesaver program
- **Preventive Medicine Specialist** (secondary MOS)
- **COP Chergotah, Khost Province, Afghanistan** — direct operational experience using this knowledge in the field

### How the Medical Disclaimer Is Implemented

Every guide with `category: 'medical'` or `requiresMedicalDisclaimer: true` renders a **non-dismissable inline disclaimer banner** as the **first visible content element**, before any procedural steps. The disclaimer reads:

> ⚕ MEDICAL INFORMATION — EMERGENCY REFERENCE ONLY  
> This guide is intended for emergency use when professional medical care is unavailable. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek qualified medical care whenever possible.

The banner is:
- **Inline** (not a modal, not a bottom sheet — cannot be skipped)
- **Non-dismissable** (no close button, no dismiss gesture)
- **Rendered before Step 1** in the guide content flow

## 2. Field Intelligence AI Feature

### What It Is

"Field Intelligence" is an on-device AI advisor built on the `llama.rn` (llama.cpp) runtime. The user picks one of two
models on first use: **Phi-3.5 Mini Instruct Q4_K_M (~2.4 GB, MIT)** or **Qwen2.5-1.5B Instruct Q4_K_M (~1.1 GB,
Apache-2.0)**; the app recommends one based on the device's RAM. Answers are grounded in the app's own survival
guides (on-device keyword retrieval) and each answer links the guides it drew from.

### Privacy & Data

- **No conversation data is transmitted to external servers.** All inference runs locally on the device.
- The model file is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) — a user-initiated, in-app-disclosed download.
  This is the only network request the feature ever makes.
- After download, the feature operates with **zero internet connection required**.
- The Offline Translator (separate feature) uses Google ML Kit; its language packs download from Google and the SDK
  registers an anonymous installation ID with Google. This is disclosed in the privacy policy and the Data Safety form.
- No conversation history is stored in the cloud, logged, or transmitted.
- No user identification is associated with queries.

### Disclosure in the App

Per Apple's 2024 AI disclosure requirement:
- Every AI-generated response is labeled **"◈ FIELD INTELLIGENCE · AI-ASSISTED"** in the message bubble.
- A persistent disclosure banner appears at the top of the chat interface: *"Field Intelligence responses are generated by an AI model running on your device. Always verify critical survival information with qualified professionals when possible."*
- The same disclosure is shown on the model download screen before the user downloads anything.

### Feature Gate

Field Intelligence is available exclusively to **Pro + AI tier** subscribers (shown in-app as "Pro + AI"; store product IDs `extreme_monthly`, `extreme_yearly`, `extreme_lifetime`).

## 3. Location Permission

GridDown requests `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` only. Location usage:
- Reads the device GPS to **display current coordinates** on the offline map screen.
- Location data is **never stored, never transmitted, never shared** with third parties.
- No background location access.
- No geofencing.

## 4. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are managed via RevenueCat (`react-native-purchases` v10).

**Compliance measures:**

- Payment disclosure: *"Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase."* — visible without scrolling.
- **Restore Purchases** button is visible at the top of the paywall without any scrolling required.
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links are at the bottom of the paywall.

**What is paid:** every guide, Emergency Mode, the offline tools, checklists, kits, flows and the Family Planner are free. **Pro** unlocks every quiz category and the HAM repeater database. **Pro + AI** adds the on-device Field Intelligence advisor. All gates are defined in one file (`app/config/access.ts`).

**Products:** Pro (monthly, yearly, lifetime) and Pro + AI (monthly, yearly, lifetime). Prices are those configured in App Store Connect; the paywall reads them live from the store.

## 5. Offline Architecture

GridDown is designed to work **100% offline** after initial content sync:
- 315 guides in 19 categories bundled as static JSON assets in the app bundle.
- Position & waypoints use the device GPS only (no map tiles, no network).
- AI model stored locally after user-initiated download.
- RevenueCat entitlement state cached locally (works offline).
- No account system. No login required. App works on first launch without any network access.

## 7. App Complexity (Guideline 4.2 — Minimum Functionality)

GridDown provides substantial standalone utility:
- 315 emergency-preparedness guides across 19 categories, each with cited public sources
- Emergency Mode: large-print crisis cards (bleeding, CPR, choking, shock, burns), SOS signaling, live GPS / MGRS
- 196 scenario quizzes, a Daily Drill and a 50-question 72-hour readiness scan
- 25 disaster checklists, 6 step-by-step Real-World Flows, kit builder, gear inventory with expiry alerts, Family Emergency Planner with printable cards
- Offline tools: HAM repeater database (218 US repeaters), radio frequencies, Morse code (audio + flashlight), position & waypoints (lat/long, UTM, MGRS), coordinate converter, star map, survival and water-purification calculators, knot guide, signaling
- On-device AI survival advisor (Phi-3.5 Mini or Qwen2.5-1.5B, fully offline after a one-time download)
- On-device translation (ML Kit, 10 languages)
- Full data export/import to a file the user keeps
